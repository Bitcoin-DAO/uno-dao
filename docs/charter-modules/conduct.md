## [n] · Conduct

1. No harassment, threats or discrimination, in [DAO name]'s activities or wherever its members meet.
2. Anyone proposing a payment says honestly what it is for, and declares any personal benefit.
3. Members do not give anyone their passkey or identity card, and tell the council if they lose their card or fear someone else is using their identity.
4. Anyone who seriously breaches this charter can be removed by a members' vote under Article 3.
