## [n] · Council handbook

1. **Checking before signing.** A council member signs a payment only after checking it in full on the screen of their own signing device.
2. **Keeping the key.** A council member never types their seed into a phone or computer, never photographs it and never shows it to anyone. They keep their seed backup, signing card and identity card in separate places.
3. **Signing rounds.** Council members take part in the payment rounds, in person or with their own device. A council member who misses more than one round without notice, or can no longer do the role, is replaced: the council proposes a council change at once, so the treasury is never left with only as many usable keys as the threshold.
4. **Members and settings.** Council members verify new members, and confirm changes to the member list and settings that the members have approved.
5. **Lost or exposed keys.** A council member who loses a key, or fears it has been seen, tells the others at once. The council proposes a council change.
6. **Stepping down.** A council member who wants to step down tells the council. The members vote on a council change, and the treasury moves to new keys before they leave.
7. **Death or incapacity.** A council member's seed belongs to [DAO name], not to the person, and does not pass to their heirs. On its own it cannot move funds. Each council member keeps a sealed note with their seed backup, without the seed, asking their family to contact the council. The council replaces the key through a council change, and asks for the backup and signing card to be destroyed in front of a council member.
