## [n] · Use of funds

1. **The funds may be used** for the purpose in Article 1, including fees and expenses for people, members or not, who work for it, and network fees.
2. **The funds may not be used** for financial investment or speculation (buying tokens, shares or other coins), loans, or any payment to a member that is not declared in the proposal. 3. Anyone proposing a payment says plainly what it is for and who benefits.
