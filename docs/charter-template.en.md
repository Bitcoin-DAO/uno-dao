This charter says what [DAO name] is for and the few principles we keep. Every member accepts it on joining.

## 1 · Purpose

[DAO name] exists to [purpose, in a few sentences: what the money is for and who it serves].

Its funding model is **[grant / production / mutual / revolving]**: [one sentence on where its money comes from and how it keeps coming]. The model is recorded in the settings (`fundingModel`), and is chosen after reading and discussing [funding models](https://github.com/Bitcoin-DAO/btc-dao/blob/main/docs/funding-models.md).

## 2 · What it is and what it is not

1. [DAO name] is a group of people organising together. It is not a company and has no legal form of its own.
2. There are no shares, tokens, dividends or investment returns. Membership gives a vote, not a claim on the funds. [Mutual DAOs: members' regular contributions are set in the settings or a charter module.]
3. A donation is a gift. It gives no rights over the funds and no extra votes.
4. No single person can spend the funds, and neither can the website or the software.

## 3 · Members

1. Anyone who shares the purpose and meets the conditions in the settings (minimum age, sponsor, verification) may be admitted by a members' vote.
2. A member may use a pseudonym. Each member has one vote, and every vote counts the same.
3. A member may leave at any time, without a vote or a reason.
4. A member may be removed by a vote. The proposal gives the reason, and the member may respond before the vote closes.

## 4 · The council

1. The council holds the treasury's keys. It carries out the members' decisions and does not decide in their place.
2. It signs only payments the members have approved. A council member may refuse to sign, but must say why, publicly.
3. The members can replace any or all of the council by a vote.

## 5 · The treasury

1. The treasury holds only bitcoin. Its balance, income and payments are public.
2. The funds can be recovered with any standard bitcoin wallet, without the website.
3. The funds are used only for the purpose in Article 1. They are never used for anything illegal, for hidden payments, or for payments the members have not approved.

## 6 · Decisions

1. Members decide by proposal and vote. Any member can make a proposal.
2. How long votes run, the quorum, which decisions need which majority, and how payments are made are set in the DAO's settings, which the members change by vote. The settings in force are always shown in the app beside this charter.
3. Discussion happens wherever the members meet. [DAO name] records the decisions.

## 7 · Public record

1. Everything [DAO name] decides is public and permanent: members' pseudonyms, rules, proposals, votes, balance and payments. Anyone can check it.
2. [DAO name] asks for the minimum: a pseudonym and a key. It does not ask for a real name, email, phone number or address.
3. On joining, every member declares that they understand and accept that their pseudonym and votes stay public forever.

## 8 · Conduct

Members act in good faith and in the interest of the purpose, treat each other with respect, and declare any personal benefit in a proposal they make. A decision taken by vote is respected; anyone who disagrees can make a new proposal.

## 9 · Changes and closing

1. This charter is changed by a members' vote, with the majority the settings require for rule changes. The settings record which text is in force, so anyone can check it.
2. [DAO name] is closed by a members' vote. The closing proposal names who receives the whole balance; after the payment, the record stays readable.
