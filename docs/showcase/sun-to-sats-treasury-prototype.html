Start with this

Bitcoin is real money you can hold yourself — no bank, no company standing between you and it. "Sats" (short for satoshis) are just its smallest unit, the way cents are to a euro. Everything on this page is built toward one thing: eventually, the value shown here becomes genuinely yours — something only you control, that nobody, including us, can freeze, take back, or lose on your behalf.

Joining costs nothing. Our solar panels power mining hardware directly, so every day the sun shines, that hardware harvests sats that flow straight into the Treasury. There's no catch and no fine print asking for your money — just a free share of what our solar powered mining already earns. If that still sounds too good to be true, good — stay skeptical, and check every step yourself using the on-chain links on this page. That's the whole point.

One thing worth knowing before you start: once Bitcoin moves, it can't be undone the way a bank transfer can. Your 12 recovery words are the only thing that controls your funds — share your wallet address freely, but never those words, with anyone, ever.

The Community Treasury

The Treasury is one shared Bitcoin wallet the whole community can see into. It's funded by the sats our solar powered mining activity earns each day, which build up here until there's enough to pay out sensibly. On a regular schedule, the Treasury sends everyone their share in a single, real Bitcoin transaction — landing straight in each member's own wallet, where only they hold the key.

Anyone can check these figures against the on-chain record. Nothing here depends on taking the platform's word for it.
