# Your first bitcoin wallet in 5 minutes

November 2026 · [Italiano](wallet-guide.it.md)

You don't need a wallet to vote in the DAO. You need one only to receive a payment: this guide takes you from nothing to a bitcoin address of your own in a few minutes, without handing your money to anyone.

## Before you start

**You need:**

- your phone, Android or iPhone;
- a sheet of paper and a pen;
- five minutes somewhere quiet, where nobody can see the screen.

**You don't need:** a bank account, ID, an email address or to buy any bitcoin. The wallet is free.

A bitcoin wallet is an app that keeps the keys to your bitcoin on your phone. No company holds them for you, and no one can freeze them. In return, the responsibility is yours: if you lose your phone, you recover everything with 12 words that you will write on paper.

## Create the wallet

Screens differ a little between apps, but the steps are always these.

1. **Install** the app from the official store and open it.
2. **Create a new wallet.** Choose "Create" or "New wallet", not "Restore". If the app asks for a network, choose Bitcoin (not Liquid, not Testnet).
3. **Write down the 12 words.** The app shows you 12 English words, in order. Write them in pen on the paper, numbered 1 to 12, checking every letter. They are the backup of the whole wallet.
4. **Check.** The app asks for some of the words to make sure you wrote them correctly. Answer by reading from the paper.
5. **Protect the app** with a PIN, fingerprint or face.
6. **Put the paper somewhere safe** at home, where no one sees it and it can't get wet. Not in your everyday wallet.

The 12 words are **never** photographed, typed into a note on your phone, sent in a chat or saved to the cloud. Anyone who reads them can take your bitcoin.

Done: you have a bitcoin wallet of your own.

## Receiving a payment from the DAO

1. **Show your address.** In the wallet tap "Receive". The app shows a bitcoin address (starting `bc1`) and its QR code.
2. **Let it be scanned** by whoever writes the proposal: they scan it into the address box, so there are no typing mistakes. If you write the proposal yourself, scan it yourself.
3. **Confirm the address.** If someone else wrote the proposal, you will get a link to it: open it, check the address is yours and tap "Confirm". Without your confirmation the payment does not go out.
4. **Wait for the round.** After the vote, the payment goes out with the monthly round, on the 1st of the month. You see it arrive in the wallet, first as "pending", then confirmed.

Use a new address for every payment: the wallet makes a new one each time you tap "Receive". Proposals are public forever, and a new address keeps this payment from being linked to your others.

## During the trial

During the trial the DAO uses testnet4, a Bitcoin test network: payments work for real, but the bitcoin are worth nothing. Test addresses start with `tb1`, not `bc1`.

- You don't need your real-bitcoin wallet yet: create it whenever you like, but don't use it for the trial.
- To receive a test payment, use a wallet that works on testnet4: Nunchuk (Android and iPhone) or Mycelium Testnet (Android), installed separately. The council will help you the first time.
- A `bc1` address in a test proposal is rejected, and so is a `tb1` address in a real one: the DAO checks the network is the right one.

## The golden rules

1. **The 12 words are your bitcoin.** Whoever has them has the money. They stay on paper, at your home.
2. **No one will ever ask for them.** Not the DAO, not the council, not the app, not a "technician". Anyone who asks is trying to rob you.
3. **Only apps from the official store,** from the right developer.
4. **Check the address** before letting it be scanned or confirming it: its start and end must match the ones in your wallet.
5. **When in doubt, ask** a council member at a meetup, not strangers in a chat.

## If you lose your phone

Your bitcoin are not in the phone: they are on the Bitcoin network, and the 12 words unlock them.

1. On a new phone, install the same app.
2. Choose "Restore" or "Import a wallet", not "Create".
3. Enter the 12 words, in the order on the paper.
4. The wallet finds all your bitcoin.

If you lose both the phone and the paper, the bitcoin are lost for good: no one can recover them. That is why the paper matters more than the phone.

For the DAO itself, your passkey or your identity card is enough: the wallet and the DAO are two separate things.
